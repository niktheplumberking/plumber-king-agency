Nick's rule

If your phone ain't ringing, I haven't done my job.

The plumber's marketing guy. Meet Nick.

Most tradesmen are brilliant at the work, but nobody ever taught them how websites, Google or online marketing work, and you shouldn't have to figure that out on your own. Too many good plumbers end up with websites that don't bring in a single enquiry, while agencies take the money and disappear.

I started Plumber King Agency because I kept seeing solid, hardworking plumbers losing out to competitors who simply had better online setups. That never sat right with me.

When you grow, I grow. If your phone starts ringing and you win better jobs, I get to show those results as a case study. I'm as invested in your success as you are, and I only want to work with plumbers who are serious about growing.

Everything I build is designed to help people trust you faster, find you easier and actually contact you. No nonsense, just honest work that helps both of us win.
