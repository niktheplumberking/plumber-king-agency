Who I am

I'm a web designer who builds brands, online stores and websites, and I now work with one trade only: plumbing. My company, Plumber King Agency, is owned by Bennett & Co LLC and serves plumbing businesses across the USA and Canada.

Most plumbers are brilliant at the work, but nobody ever taught them how websites, Google or online marketing work. Too many good plumbers end up with websites that don't bring in a single enquiry, while agencies take the money and disappear. I started Plumber King Agency to change that.

When you work with me, you deal with me directly. No account managers, no call centre, no jargon.
