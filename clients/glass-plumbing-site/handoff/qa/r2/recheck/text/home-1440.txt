Do I need a permit to replace a water heater in Pinellas?
WATER HEATERS

Usually, yes. Pinellas County lists a “Water Heater Equal Change Out” permit for unincorporated areas, Clearwater requires permits for water heaters, and Largo issues its own. Ask us on the call and we’ll tell you what yours needs.
