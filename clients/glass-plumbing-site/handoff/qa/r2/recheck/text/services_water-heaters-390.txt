If the tank is dry and the problem is a part, like an element, thermostat or valve, a repair is usually enough. A tank that leaks usually needs replacing. A replacement usually needs a permit: Pinellas County lists a “Water Heater Equal Change Out, Tank or Tankless” express permit for unincorporated areas, and Clearwater requires water heater permits. In Largo or another city, check with the city’s permit office.

WHAT’S WRONG WITH IT?

Six ways a water heater
tells you something’s wrong.
01
No hot water

Element, pilot or thermostat. Often a part, not the tank.

02
Runs out fast

Often a failing element, or a tank too small for the house.

03
Rusty water

Often the anode rod is used up.

04
Leaking tank

If the tank shell leaks, it usually needs replacing.

05
Rumbling and popping

Scale at the bottom. Pinellas water averages 217 ppm.

06
Relief valve dripping

A safety part. For a licensed plumber.

Tank or tankless for a Florida home?

It depends on how much hot water you use at once and whether you have gas. Tankless heats on demand and hangs on the wall. A tank stores hot water and usually costs less to fit.
