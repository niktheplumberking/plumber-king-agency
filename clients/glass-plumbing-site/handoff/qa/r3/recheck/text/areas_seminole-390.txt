Which office issues my permit?
City or County
In short: City or County.
It depends on the address: your city or the county. The City of Seminole has its own Building Division for addresses inside the city; unincorporated Pinellas goes to Pinellas County. Not sure which you are? We tell you on the call.
Is the water hard here?
217 ppm
In short: 217 ppm.
Pinellas County Utilities water averages 217 ppm hardness, or 11.1 to 14.3 grains per gallon (2025 report). Your water bill shows which utility serves your home.
