01 · EMERGENCIES
A pipe burst. What do I do first?

Close your main shut-off valve to stop the water, then call (727) 512-0673.

Do you really answer 24/7?

Yes. For emergencies, call (727) 512-0673 any hour, day or night.

Where is my main shut-off valve?

Usually where the water line enters the house, or at the meter near the street. Find it and label it before you need it, as Pinellas County advises.

Source · Pinellas County, “Prepare your pipes for cold weather”

My water heater sat in storm flood water. Can I use it?

Don’t turn it on. A heater that sat in flood water, salt water above all, usually needs replacing. Have a licensed plumber look at it before you use it.

02 · WATER HEATERS
Tank or tankless for a Florida home?

It depends on how much hot water you use at once and whether you have gas. Tankless heats on demand and hangs on the wall. A tank stores hot water and usually costs less to fit.

Do I need a permit to replace a water heater?

Usually, yes. Pinellas County lists a “Water Heater Equal Change Out, Tank or Tankless” express permit for unincorporated areas, and Clearwater requires permits for water heaters. Largo and other cities: check with the city’s permit office.

Source · pinellas.gov Express Permits · myclearwater.com, read 17 Nov 2026

Source · Kevin Kelleman, Google review

03 · HARD WATER
How hard is Pinellas County water?

Very hard. It averages 217 ppm, which is 11.1 to 14.3 grains per gallon. That builds scale inside tanks and tankless units.

Source · Pinellas County Utilities 2025 water quality report

What should my softener be set to?

Pinellas County Utilities gives 11.1 to 14.3 grains per gallon as the softener setting. A softener is usually set to that range when it is installed.

Source · Pinellas County Utilities 2025 water quality report

Why does my water smell like a pool some weeks?

Twice a year Pinellas County Utilities switches from chloramine to free chlorine for a short time. A pool smell in those weeks is usually that switch, not your pipes.

Source · pinellas.gov water quality pages

04 · PERMITS IN PINELLAS
Which office issues my permit?

It depends on your address. Unincorporated areas go to Pinellas County, Clearwater takes permits online, and Largo has its own Building Division. If you are not sure which applies, ask your city.

Source · largo.com · myclearwater.com · pinellas.gov, read 17 Nov 2026

Does a repipe need a permit?

Usually, yes. Pinellas County lists a “Re-pipe Water Distribution Interior Only” permit for unincorporated areas. Inside a city, check with that city’s permit office.

Source · pinellas.gov Express Permits, read 17 Nov 2026
