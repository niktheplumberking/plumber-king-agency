02 · WATER HEATERS
Tank or tankless for a Florida home?

It depends on how much hot water you use at once and whether you have gas. Tankless heats on demand and hangs on the wall. A tank stores hot water and usually costs less to fit.

03 · HARD WATER
How hard is Pinellas County water?

Very hard. It averages 217 ppm, which is 11.1 to 14.3 grains per gallon. That builds scale inside tanks and tankless units.

Source · Pinellas County Utilities 2025 water quality report

04 · PERMITS IN PINELLAS
Which office issues my permit?

It depends on your address. Unincorporated areas go to Pinellas County, Clearwater takes permits online, and Largo has its own Building Division. If you are not sure which applies, ask your city.

Source · largo.com · myclearwater.com · pinellas.gov, read 17 Nov 2026
