Facts as of 17 Nov 2026 (sources: handoff/research/BRAND-AUDIT.md, clients/mikes-plumbing/research/FACTS-CHECK.md).

## 1. Google Business Profile (most important: the #1 local ranking factor)
- Create it as a service-area business. Base: Riverside, CA. Hide the street address. Add the service areas: Riverside, Corona, Moreno Valley, Murrieta, Buena Park, Los Angeles and West Hollywood (or Mike's final list).
- Primary category: Plumber. Secondary categories only for services he really does: Drainage service, Water heater installation service, Sewage disposal service (check each one with Mike).
- Business name exactly "Mike's Plumbing Services". Use phone (951) 809-9533 everywhere. Add the website and the 15% off offer as a post.
- Hours: only what Mike confirms. Yelp says open 24 hours, Nextdoor says 7 AM to 11 PM.
- Photos: the real job photos already on his Instagram, his logo and his van.
- Verification is Mike's step (video or postcard).

## 2. NAP citations (name, address and phone, identical everywhere)
Order: fix the Nextdoor URL slug and city (Riverside, not Irvine, if Riverside is the base), then Apple Business Connect, Bing Places (it can import from Google), BBB (free listing, no paid accreditation needed), Angi, Thumbtack, HomeAdvisor, Houzz, Nextdoor, and the Greater Riverside Chambers of Commerce member directory (paid membership: Nick decides).
Every listing should carry the same name, the same phone, the same website and "Riverside, CA" as a service area.

## 3. Reviews
- Once the Google profile exists, send a short review link (g.page/r/...) to every finished job, by text and on the job invoice.
- Ask each past Yelp reviewer only by personal message, never in a bulk send. Yelp forbids asking for reviews, so never ask for Yelp reviews.
- Never offer anything for a review: no discounts, no gifts.
- Reply to every review within a few days, using his own voice.

## 4. Local links (real ones only)
- Suppliers he buys from (local plumbing supply houses). Many have a "find a plumber" or contractor page.
- Community: sponsor a Riverside youth team or event, which usually comes with a link on the sponsor page.
- Local news: "new family business" features in local outlets (The Press-Enterprise, Patch Riverside). This is only a pitch, nothing is guaranteed.
- Instagram: the bio link points to the new site, and posts link to the matching service page.

## 5. Never
No paid links, no link exchanges, no PBNs, no mass-produced town pages beyond the towns he actually serves, and no fake reviews.

## 6. Measure (monthly, skill pka-client-site step 7)
- Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster: submit sitemap-index.xml at launch.
- Google Business Profile insights: calls and direction requests.
- Count of reviews on Google and Yelp.
- Rankings for "plumber Riverside CA" plus each town and service.
