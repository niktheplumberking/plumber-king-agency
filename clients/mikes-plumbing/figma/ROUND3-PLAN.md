## 1. Design language: "Riverside workshop ledger"
Story: a family trade done by hand. Mike grew up in his family's grease-trade business, learned the dirty work, now
runs his own badge. Everything looks like it came off a craftsman's bench: engraved seals, stamped tickets, brass
tags, ledger pages, signatures. Never generic cards.
- Seal: round engraved seal, text set around the ring ("20 YELP REVIEWS · ALL FIVE STARS · RIVERSIDE CA ·"), big centre figure.
- Ticket: cream work-order paper, perforated edge (punched circles), ruled rows, tick boxes, Yellowtail handwriting.
- Brass tag: tag shape with punched hole and string, used for trust facts (Insured, Family owned, Free estimates, CSLB to confirm).
- Ledger: thin ruled rows inside a column only (never full-width page lines), dotted leaders "Riverside ...... home base".
- Stamp: tan outline circle, rotated, "DONE RIGHT · CLEANED UP".
- Keep (praised): hex badge buttons, cropped watermark word, slanted edges, "Ready when you are" contact.
- Palette only his: ink #0F0E0C, char, cream #E8E3C8, paper #F4F0E1, tan #D2C4A7, tan dark #8E8268. Fonts: Playfair Display / Jost / Yellowtail.
