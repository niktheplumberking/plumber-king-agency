# Mike's Plumbing Services: design system (direction A "The Badge", draft until Nick picks)
Brief: the website is Mike's badge: black and cream like his logo and flyer, stamped like a craftsman's mark.

## Colour tokens (WCAG computed 17 Nov)
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| black | #111111 | lead dark field |
| char | #232323 | second dark field, cards on black |
| cream | #E9E3C9 | text + outlines on dark (14.65 on black, 12.2 on char) |
| paper | #F4F0E2 | light field (ink on paper 15.54) |
| tan | #D8CFB0 | panels, emergency device (black on tan 12.11) |
| muted | #A39C86 | small labels on dark only (6.89 on black, 5.73 on char) |
| ink | #1A1814 | text on light fields |
Banned pairs: cream on tan (1.21), muted on tan (1.76), muted on paper. No blue, red, yellow, glow or gradient glow (United/Glass/Khinda).
Field budget per page: dark ~55-60%, paper/tan ~40%; never the same field twice in a row.

## Type (3 voices)
| Voice | Font | Use | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Display + body | Playfair Display 900 (H1 64-72 desktop, 40 mobile; H2 48 / 32) + Jost 400/500/600 (body 17/26, lead 20/30) | headings two-line two-tone (cream + tan, or ink + muted-dark) | Playfair for body text |
| 2 Accent | Playfair Display 900 italic, 1-3 words | one word per heading max | script fonts (Yellowtail is Paul's) |
| 3 Labels | Playfair Display SC 700, tracked +18%, 12-13 px | eyebrows ("EST 2025" style), stamps, numbers | long text |

Headline pattern: eyebrow (hex tick + small caps) / two-line two-tone H2 / lead; alignment alternates left, centred, left, split.

## Motif: "the badge" (min 6 uses per long page)
1. Hex-cut photo frames (elongated hexagon, corner cut 28 px desktop / 16 px mobile), max 3 per page.
2. Badge divider: 1.5 px cream (or ink) rule that kinks into the badge's angled ends at the centre of each section join. Every join on dark/light changes.
3. Stamp eyebrows: small hex outline + small caps label.
4. Tan siren panel (from his flyer) = the emergency device only.
5. Motto pillars "Friendly. / Transparent. / Proficient." (home + about only, different layouts).
Never: sun, heart, LED dots, waves, pipe runs, wires, halftone, tickers, name patches.

## Photos
Real IG/Yelp only, warm grade (+contrast, slight warm), never twice on one page, nothing over faces. Hero = van + drain machine photo in a hex-cut frame.
