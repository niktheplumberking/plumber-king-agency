# Mike's Plumbing Services: 3 design directions + translation table (pka-site-craft steps 2-3, 17 Nov 2026)
Structure = approved wireframe (unchanged). This is only the look.

## A. "The Badge" (RECOMMENDED)
- Idea: the website is Mike's logo badge and flyer: black and cream, framed like a craftsman's stamp.
- Colour: warm black #111 / charcoal #232323 lead, cream #E9E3C9 type, tan #D8CFB0 panels; dark and tan sections alternate. No glow, no third colour.
- Type: Playfair Display Black (logo voice) headings; Jost body; tracked small caps (logo "EST 2025" voice) for eyebrows and labels. No script.
- Motif "the badge" (owned, 6+ uses per long page): elongated-hex frames on photos and number tiles; a cream rule that kinks into the badge's angled ends as the divider at each section join; "EST 2025"-style stamp eyebrows; the flyer's tan siren panel as the emergency device.
- Photos: real IG/Yelp job photos in hex-cut frames, one warm grade; van + drain camera shots big.
- Signature idea: his own motto "Friendly. Transparent. Proficient." as three badge pillars, each proven by a real Yelp line
  (Friendly: Jackie H. "working from the heart"; Transparent: Sal C. "upfront and honest with me on pricing";
  Proficient: Stuart W. diagnosis the same night). Second proof device: "Left it clean" (7 of 20 reviews say clean).
- Risk: dark-led like United; fixed by warm black + cream (United is cold navy + neon cyan/yellow with glow).

## B. "Spotless" (clean job site)
- Light cream-paper led, black type, big before/after photos, the "clean" reviews as the spine.
- Risk: cream-led matches Glass's field; needs before/after photo pairs Mike may not have.

## C. "Family Trade" (grease-industry roots)
- Charcoal + tan workwear labels, stencil-like numerals, timeline of the family trade.
- Risk: the family story is 2 lines on Yelp (thin proof); stencil feel is generic; needs family photos that don't exist yet.

Pick: A. Built from what he owns (badge, flyer, motto), answers "reads like a manual" with a strong visual voice, uses proof
nobody else has (his own motto backed by his reviews), needs no new assets.
