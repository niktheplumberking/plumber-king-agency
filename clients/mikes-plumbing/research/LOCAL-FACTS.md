## 1. Plumbing permits, City of Riverside

- **FACT: Issuing department.** City of Riverside, Community & Economic Development Department, Building & Safety Division, 3900 Main Street, 3rd Floor, Riverside, CA 92522, 951-826-5697, RiversideCA.gov/Building.
  Source (letterhead of city bulletins): http://riversideca.gov/cedd/sites/riversideca.gov.cedd/files/pdf/building/forms/Inspections_page_handouts/Sewer%20Installation%20Requirements.pdf
- **FACT: When a plumbing permit is needed.** The city's permits page says permits are required to "erect, install, enlarge, alter, repair, remove, convert or replace any electrical, gas, mechanical or plumbing system, the installation of which is regulated by code." Plumbing is one of its four permit types. Permit Counter: (951) 826-5800.
  Source: https://riversideca.gov/cedd/building-safety/building-safety-services/permits
- **FACT: Water heater replacement.** Same page: simple water heater change-outs can be issued the same day, over the counter.
  Source: https://riversideca.gov/cedd/building-safety/building-safety-services/permits
- **FACT: State rule behind it (applies in every CA city).** California Plumbing Code 502.1: "It shall be unlawful for a person to install, remove, or replace or cause to be installed, removed, or replaced a water heater without first obtaining a permit from the Authority Having Jurisdiction to do so."
  Source: https://gocodebook.com/library/us/ca/ca-plumbing-code/chapter-5-water-heaters/502.1-general.-it-shall-be-unlawful-for-a-person-to-install
- **FACT: Repiping.** No repipe-specific page found; it falls under the general rule above (altering/replacing a plumbing system needs a permit).
  Source: https://riversideca.gov/cedd/building-safety/building-safety-services/permits
- **FACT: Sewer line repair.** City Bulletin 250 "Sewer Installation Requirements": a property-line cleanout is required "when the existing building sewer is replaced or repaired at the property line"; building sewers must be at least 12 inches below ground; the replaced sewer line and cleanout must stay accessible for inspection before backfill; transition couplings must be listed and labelled (a common failed inspection is non-approved couplings).
  Source: http://riversideca.gov/cedd/sites/riversideca.gov.cedd/files/pdf/building/forms/Inspections_page_handouts/Sewer%20Installation%20Requirements.pdf
- **FACT: Water softeners (Bulletin 216).** Enforced by Building & Safety: softeners must discharge to the sanitary sewer with a 1-inch air gap, use demand-controlled regeneration, and be certified by a contractor with a C-55 or C-36 (plumbing) licence at final inspection.
  Source: http://riversideca.gov/cedd/sites/riversideca.gov.cedd/files/pdf/building/forms/216_RESI%20WATER%20SOFTNER_V1.0.pdf
- Other official links: Public Permit Portal https://riversideca.gov/cedd/building-safety/online-services/public-permit-portal ; Forms & handouts https://riversideca.gov/cedd/building-safety/forms-handouts-bulletins
- **NOT FOUND:** a City of Riverside handout specific to water heaters or repiping (the forms page lists none).

## 2. Water hardness, Riverside (Riverside Public Utilities)

- **FACT: Latest report = RPU 2025 Water Quality Report** (data from sampling in calendar year 2025).
  Report: https://www.riversideca.gov/utilities/sites/riversideca.gov.utilities/files/pdf/water-quality/2025%20Water%20Quality%20Report.pdf
  Listed on: https://riversideca.gov/utilities/residents/our-water/water-quality
- **FACT: Hardness (as CaCO3): average 197 mg/L = 12 grains per gallon; range 180-210 mg/L** (2025). Magnesium 9 mg/L.
- **FACT: Water source:** RPU met all 2025 supply from groundwater in the Bunker Hill and Riverside Basins, blended centrally before distribution.
- **FACT: Lead/galvanized:** RPU says it has replaced all its known lead service lines, but "older homes (those typically built before 1986) may still have a privately owned lead or galvanized line", which is the customer's responsibility. In 2025, 51 homes were tested; no lead detected at the 90th percentile.
  Source for all three: the 2025 report PDF above.

## 3. Common local home plumbing problems (sourced only)

- **FACT: Hard water / scale.** RPU water averages 12 gpg (197 mg/L) hardness (see section 2). Source: RPU 2025 report PDF above.
- **FACT: Older private service lines.** Pre-1986 homes may still have private lead or galvanized service lines (RPU 2025 report, above).
- **FACT: Tree roots in sewer laterals / homeowner responsibility.** City Public Works brochure: care and maintenance of the sewer lateral and cleanout from the house to the property line is the homeowner's responsibility; if a lateral is blocked, the homeowner must find the cause and clear it, and install a property-line cleanout if there isn't one; roots "will seek the moist nutrient rich soil and may enter a failed sewer lateral" and can block flow and back up drains; never pour fats, oils or grease down the drain; don't plant trees or shrubs near the lateral. It notes "most licensed plumbers can provide service to locate and clear a lateral, install a clean out and make necessary repairs."
  Source: https://riversideca.gov/publicworks/sites/riversideca.gov.publicworks/files/pdf/2017%20Home%20Sewer%20Line%20Maintenence%20Brochure.PDF
- **FACT: Old neighbourhoods exist** (useful for "older pipes" copy, but no source states their pipe material): the Eastside "has been a part of the city since its founding in 1870"; Canyon Crest has a 1910 Craftsman bungalow on the National Register. Source: city neighbourhood list (section 4).
- **NOT FOUND:** a Riverside-specific source on slab leaks / slab foundations being common (only the softener bulletin mentions "homes with slab foundations" in passing). Do not claim slab leaks are common in Riverside without a source.
- **NOT FOUND:** a source giving the pipe materials (galvanized, cast iron, etc.) in specific Riverside neighbourhoods.

## 5. Nearby towns: permits + water hardness (short)

General rule for all: CPC 502.1 requires a permit to install, remove or replace a water heater (source in section 1).

### Corona
- **FACT, permits:** City of Corona Building Division, 400 S. Vicentia Ave., Suite 120, 951-736-2250, Mon-Thu 7am-6pm (closed Fri); permits through eTrakit https://etrakit.coronaca.gov/etrakit/ . - **NOT FOUND:** a Corona city page stating the water heater / repipe / sewer permit rule in its own words.
- **FACT, hardness:** 2025 CCR: system average 146 ppm = 8.5 grains per gallon, "classified as hard"; 5,150 tests in 2025. Source: https://www.coronaca.gov/departments/utilities/about-dwp/consumer-confidence-report

### Moreno Valley
- **FACT, permits:** City of Moreno Valley Community Development, Building & Safety; permits via SimpliCITY (moval.gov/simplicity). Water Heater permit (BOW) covers "all types of water heaters to include electric and tankless"; Plumbing permit (BOP) covers new/alteration/relocation "to include like for like change outs." Source: https://moval.gov/cdd/services/permits-new.html
- **NOT FOUND:** Moreno Valley repipe/sewer permit wording; Building & Safety phone on that page.
- **FACT, hardness:** Eastern Municipal Water District (EMWD) 2025 CCR lists Moreno Valley in the Mills Service Area. Hardness: Mills Filtration Plant water 4.8-5.5 gpg (avg 5.1); Perris Filtration Plant 4.7-16 gpg (avg 7.5); Desalination Complex 4.1-8.9 gpg (avg 6.3). Source: https://content.emwd.org/sites/default/files/2026-06/2025%20CCR%20FINAL%20-%20for%20web_2.pdf (linked from https://www.emwd.org/ccr)
