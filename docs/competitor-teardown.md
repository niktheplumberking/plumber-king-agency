## Type scale they actually use (desktop)
H1 65 / H2 43 / sub-H 23 / card H3 26-29 / body 15-17 / small 12. One family (Inter), weight 800 for heads, 400 body. Emphasis = second line of the H2 in red italic, same size.

## Rules to carry over
1. Section 2 is proof, not features. They lead with reviews right after the hero.
2. The founder's face is the trust engine (sections 3 and 6). Our hero should lead with Nick's photo.
3. No hard horizontal seams: logo strips sit on the hero base, a video overlaps a card edge, badges overlap photo corners, cards float on dark.
4. Stickers and badges are scattered with freedom (quote pills around the mascot, gold seal on photo corner).
5. Light and dark alternate, but each change is bridged by an overlapping element.
6. One CTA wording repeated everywhere ("Free custom design") plus a low-commitment alternative (7-minute video).

## Our constraint (honest)
We have no real reviews, client logos or case numbers yet. We must not invent them. Our section 2 has to carry trust another way: Nick himself, a guarantee, and a "show, don't tell" demo of what we'd build for the visitor's own company. Real reviews slot in the moment Nick has them.
