Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how Plumber King Agency, a business of Bennett & Co LLC (“we”, “us”), handles personal information collected through this website.

1. Information we collect

We collect the information you give us when you fill out a form, request a strategy call or website audit, or email us. This may include your name, business name, email address, phone number and website address. Our web host may also keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) for security and reliability. When you book a call, the booking is handled by Calendly, which processes the details you enter under its own privacy policy.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide the services you ask for.
  • To prepare your website audit or strategy call.
  • To send you marketing messages, only where you have agreed to receive them. You can opt out at any time.
  • To improve our website and services, and to comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business (such as hosting, email and customer-management tools), under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

4. Use of artificial intelligence

We work with artificial intelligence (AI). Our website, content and services are produced and delivered with the help of AI tools. Information you send us (for example through our forms, by email or on a call) may be processed with AI tools to help us understand your request, prepare proposals and deliver your project. Those tools are provided by third-party service providers and are used under their own terms.

Images, videos and text on this website may be generated or edited by AI, and people, businesses and results shown in examples may be AI-generated rather than real. AI makes mistakes, so AI-assisted content can contain errors. If you notice something wrong, please tell us at the address below.

5. Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of sending data over the internet is completely secure. We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to keep it working and to understand how visitors use it. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California or Canada), you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of marketing. To make a request, email us at the address below.

8. Children

This website is meant for businesses and is not directed at children under 16.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: nick@plumberkingagency.com, or write to Bennett & Co LLC, 30 N Gould St Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA.
